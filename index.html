How it works

Your steps are the key

1

Pick your apps

Choose the apps that steal your time — social, video, games. WalkFirst locks them with Apple's Screen Time framework.

2

Set a step goal

Pick a daily goal that fits your life. Steps sync automatically from Apple Health — no manual logging.

3

Walk to earn time

Hit 50% of your goal and earn 10 minutes. 75% earns 15. The app tells you exactly how many steps buy your next unlock.

4

Hit 100% — unlimited

Reach your full goal and your apps are yours for the rest of the day. Tomorrow, the walk starts again.

Earn every minute

Every step buys screen time

Block Instagram, X, TikTok — any app you choose. The list shows exactly what's locked and how many steps buy your next unlock: "174 steps to 50% — earns 10 min."

Your pace

Goals, streaks & achievements

Set a daily step goal that fits your life. Keep your streak alive, chase achievements, and adjust everything — goal, blocking schedule, units — in seconds.

Progress & streaks

Numbers that keep you honest

Weekly, monthly and yearly charts with daily averages, goal rate, and personal bests. Watching the average climb is half the motivation.

Private by design

Everything stays on your iPhone

Steps come from Apple Health. Blocking runs through Apple's Screen Time. There's no account, no server, and your data never leaves the device.
